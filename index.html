Common questions

The reminder did not appear.
Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's settings. On Android, also turn off battery optimisation for the app (Settings → Apps → Hizbul A'zam → Battery → Unrestricted) and, for exact timing, allow "Alarms and reminders". The app's Reminder tab has a "Send a test notification" button to check.
How do I make the text bigger?
Use the A− / A+ buttons at the bottom of any reading page, or the slider in Settings.
Can I change the background colour or the Arabic typeface?
Yes: the Colour button cycles through the backgrounds, and Settings lets you choose the typeface and whether source references are shown.
How do the audio buttons work?
Tap a du'a, then the play icon, to hear that du'a. The play icon in the top bar reads the whole day. The bundled audio is a computer-generated voice; it is there to help with pronunciation, not as a substitute for recitation from a qari.
Is anything sent to the internet?
No. The app has no accounts, analytics or adverts and works fully offline. See the privacy policy.
I found a mistake in the Arabic.
Please email the day, the du'a number and the correction; the text is checked against the printed book and updated in the next release.

About the book

Al-Hizbul-A'zam (Abridged) was compiled by Hadrat Sufi Iqbal Madani under the instruction of Shaykhul-Hadith Hadrat Mawlana Muhammad Zakariyya (rahimahumallah), from Al-Hizbul-A'zam of Mulla 'Ali al-Qari (rahimahullah). The printed book, with an English translation, is published by At-Tazkiyah and available from the Islamic Da'wah Academy, Leicester.
